What Sets My Work Apart

Most instructional designers focus on knowledge transfer. I focus on behavior change. The distinction matters because knowing what to do and actually doing it in high-pressure, real-world contexts are fundamentally different challenges.

  • Evidence-Based Design: Every decision I make is grounded in cognitive science research, not just industry trends or personal preference.
  • Technical Versatility: I'm equally comfortable building custom JavaScript interactions as I am designing in Storyline. Technology serves the learning goal, not the other way around.
  • Cross-Sector Perspective: 15 years across six industries means I bring proven solutions from one context to solve problems in another.
  • Performance-First Mindset: I design for what happens after the training, not just during it. Job aids, microlearning, and performance support are often better solutions than traditional courses.
